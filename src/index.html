Engineering Approach

Builds are presented as decisions, not just finished objects.

01

Define the constraint

Clarify the function, material limits, size, budget, tools, and success criteria.

02

Prototype the system

Model, fabricate, wire, assemble, and revise until the design becomes testable.

03

Explain the result

Show what worked, what changed, and what the next version would improve.
