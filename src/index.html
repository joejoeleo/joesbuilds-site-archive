About

I like turning design decisions into physical prototypes.

I’m drawn to engineering work that moves from sketch to model to physical prototype. I like the point where a design has to survive real materials, real tolerances, real wiring, and real use.

The projects here come from school, personal work, and independent experimenting. Together they show the way I approach design: make it useful, make it clean, and keep improving it until it works.
